Data Deletion Instructions

Aarix Auto DM

You can remove your data at any time:

  1. In the app, open Connect and tap Disconnect. This clears the data saved in your browser.
  2. In Instagram, go to Settings → Apps and websites and remove Aarix Auto DM. This revokes the app’s access to your account.
  3. To delete the data held on our server (your stored access token, automation rules and activity log), send a message to @techaarix on Instagram from the account you connected, with the words “delete my data”. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

See also our Privacy Policy.
