Privacy Policy

Aarix Auto DM · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Aarix Auto DM (“the app”, “we”) lets Instagram creators set up automatic replies: when someone comments a chosen keyword on a creator’s post or reel, the app sends that commenter a direct message. This page explains what data the app handles.

Data we receive

  • When you connect Instagram: your Instagram account ID, username, name and profile picture, and the list of your posts and reels (caption, like and comment counts, thumbnail).
  • When someone comments on your content: the comment text, the commenter’s Instagram ID and username, and the comment ID. We use these only to decide whether a rule matches and to send the reply you configured.
  • When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture, used to show your account in the app.

Data we store

  • An access token for your Instagram account, stored encrypted on our server so the app can send the replies you set up.
  • The automation rules you create (keywords and message text).
  • A short activity log (for example “comment received”, “message sent”, or an error), kept only for troubleshooting.

Comment text is not stored beyond a short excerpt in that log.

How we use data

Only to provide the automation you ask for. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to build profiles.

Sharing

Data is processed through Meta’s Instagram platform and hosted on Cloudflare and GitHub Pages infrastructure. We do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect Instagram in the app at any time, and remove the app under Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites, which revokes its access. To have the data we hold deleted, follow the steps on our data deletion page.

Children

The app is not intended for people under 13.

Contact

Questions or requests: message @techaarix on Instagram.
